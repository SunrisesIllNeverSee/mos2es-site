About

Measure the people who use AI. Govern the agents. Keep the commitments.

MO§ES™ is the umbrella for everything Ello Cello builds. One theory underneath, four products on top.

The idea

Models get benchmarked constantly. The people operating them don't. And when AI summarizes, compresses or hands work between agents, nobody checks whether what was committed survives the trip.

Commitment Theory says it should, and shows how to measure it. SignalAF and SigRank measure the operator. Signomy governs the agents. AQUA keeps your own answers reusable and traceable.

What's shipped

  • SignalAF. The live leaderboard: profiles, history, head-to-head comparisons, achievements and the Hall of Signal.
  • SigRank. CLI, MCP server and TUI on npm, with signed submissions. Listed in the official MCP Registry as com.signalaf/sigrank.
  • Claude plugin. The SignalAF Analyst plugin brings the board into Claude.
  • Live telemetry. An OpenTelemetry receiver and live dashboard at signalaf.com/otel.
  • TTEOP. An open interoperability spec for operator telemetry (DOI).
  • Signomy and AQUA. A governed agent marketplace and a reusable application answer bank, both live.

The founder

Deric J. McHenry built DJMP Inc., a contracting company, from zero to roughly $1M a year with a team of up to forty, and has run it since 2011. He learned to sell, schedule, deliver and carry operating risk before writing software. He studied sociology and history at the University at Buffalo.

He started working with AI in July 2025 and went full time that October. Before writing code he staked out the ground: the company, the design, the architecture, patent filings and the papers. He began coding in March 2026 and built the research, products and public surfaces with Claude Code, Codex and Devin, without outside engineers.
