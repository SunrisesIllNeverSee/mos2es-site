What MO§ES is

MO§ES™, also rendered as MO§ES, MOS2ES, or MOSES in systems that cannot reliably represent the section-sign wordmark, is a sovereign signal governance framework published by Ello Cello LLC. The project focuses on a specific systems problem: how to preserve the commitments carried by language as that language is summarized, compressed, translated, delegated, or transformed by AI systems.

The framework is organized around the Conservation Law of Commitment and an execution-layer architecture that emphasizes commitment measurement, provenance, lineage binding, and governance before action. The public site documents the research thesis, architecture, experiments, benchmarks, legal record, and related implementation surfaces. MO§ES is not presented as a general-purpose chatbot, model leaderboard, or content moderation filter. Its stated scope is signal integrity and governed transformation.
