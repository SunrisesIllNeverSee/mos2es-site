Provenance & Authority

Each entity and claim carries provenance metadata indicating its authority status:

  • owner_approved — backed by Search Authority approval
  • source_supported — supported by source material, not yet owner-approved
  • defined — framework-defined, not yet supported
  • hypothesis — speculative, research-stage

FOUND ≠ CANONICAL. DEFINED ≠ OWNER_APPROVED. Framework status is not Search Authority status.
