The ideas behind it

Five concepts, in the order they matter.

01

Recursive Compression

Every summary, rewrite or handoff transforms a signal. Repeat it and commitment can leak away. This is the T in the Conservation Law.

 02

Governance Enforcement

Gates, thresholds and lineage checks that run before a transformation. With them, the law says commitment holds. Without them, it degrades.

 03

Constitutional Substrate

The layer where the rules live: what may change, what must hold, and what gets checked before anything runs.

 04

Signal Encoding

Writes commitment into the signal itself, so meaning can be measured and verified, not just observed after the fact.

 05

Origin Binding

Ties every artifact to the cycle that produced it with a cryptographic hash, so copies and mimics can't pass as the original.
