Scope

This privacy notice applies to the public website at mos2es.com, published by Ello Cello LLC. The site primarily provides research, architecture, benchmark, legal, press, and product information about MO§ES™. It does not require visitors to create an account to read the public pages, and the public site is not intended to collect sensitive personal information, authentication secrets, regulated records, or confidential production data.

Analytics and technical data

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic and page usage. As with ordinary web hosting and analytics, requests may produce technical information such as approximate location, device and browser characteristics, referring page, requested URL, timestamps, and network identifiers used to deliver and secure the site. Ello Cello LLC uses this information to understand which public materials are useful, diagnose site problems, maintain security, and improve navigation and documentation.

Information you choose to send

If you contact the publisher by email, the message and contact information you provide are received for the purpose of responding to that inquiry and maintaining necessary business records. Do not send passwords, API keys, private model prompts, regulated personal data, or other secrets through the public contact channel. Initial research and collaboration inquiries should use public references, repository links, DOI identifiers, or non-confidential descriptions whenever possible.

External services and links

mos2es.com links to third-party services including GitHub, Zenodo, ORCID, Signomy, SignalAF, and other public resources. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Following an external link leaves this site. The presence of a link documents provenance, related work, or a public resource; it does not make Ello Cello LLC the controller of data collected by the destination service.
