04Problem
The human side of AI performance

We obsess over model performance
and ranking. Why not those who actually use it?

Models
Benchmarks, evals, rankings and leaderboards for every release.
Operators
Two people on the same model get very different results, and nothing records that difference.
Today’s tools
Seat analytics count usage. Frequency of use is not proficiency. Every vendor counts tokens its own way, so nothing compares across platforms.
Companies are buying AI seats for everyone. No one can tell who is using them well.
05Product
Measure + compare

SigRank measures. SignalAF makes it public.

How it works
Install the CLI, MCP server or Claude plugin. It reads your AI sessions locally across 22 platforms. Only counts leave the device. Four token pillars become five stats, a profile, a class tier and an archetype. Then you compare: boards, head-to-head, history.
One standard for every metric across all 22 platforms, measured from what the operator typed.
