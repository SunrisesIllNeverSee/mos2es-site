02The Gap
Ello Cello

Models are benchmarked.
Operators are not.

People establish intent, supply context, correct errors, and build on prior interactions. Those actions change outcomes, but most enterprise tooling still measures models, seats, or raw activity.
ActionWhat breaks today
CountToken volume records activity. It does not tell you whether the operator produced useful yield.
JudgeModel evals test the tool. They do not isolate the human contribution to the human-AI pair.
CompareTeams cannot compare operators across tools without either losing semantics or exposing private content.
ImproveWithout a baseline and remeasurement loop, training and AI transformation cannot prove what changed.
Tokens are the instrument. Human-AI performance is the object.
