Research, technical, and press requests

Researchers and technical teams should cite the relevant DOI or GitHub repository when requesting clarification or reproducibility details. Press inquiries should identify the intended publication and deadline. Potential collaborators should describe the workflow, governance problem, or research question rather than sending confidential production data in the first message. The public Papers & Proofs, Legal & IP, Press, and About pages contain the current reference material.

Privacy

Do not send passwords, API keys, regulated personal data, or other secrets by email. For information about analytics and website data handling, review the Privacy page before submitting information through any public channel.
